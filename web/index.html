The MedGift Manifesto: Reuse, Empower, Heal the Planet

Every year, thousands of fully functional medical devices are left unused in storage, while millions of individuals with disabilities lack access to essential mobility equipment. MedGift US bridges this gap by turning unused medical items into life-changing mobility solutions.

Stop Overconsumption

We end the cycle of waste by reclaiming usable equipment and connecting it directly with those in need — stopping overconsumption at its root.

Empowered Community

A global, transparent solidarity network built by and for the disability community. We break down financial barriers and offer dignified, peer-to-peer access to mobility tools, ensuring no one is left behind.

Clean Energy & ESG

Medical equipment recycling is clean energy in action. By extending the lifecycle of existing items, we cut factory emissions, reduce electronic and plastic waste, and track real-time CO2 savings on our live ESG dashboard.
